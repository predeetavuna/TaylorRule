⏱ Data notes & known limitations — UK build sourced 2026-08-04
Sample start: 1988 Q1
CPI (ONS D7BT) is the binding constraint — it doesn't exist before 1988, later than every other input.
Debt/GDP, 1988–99: bridged, not real Maastricht data
Maastricht debt (ONS ESA Table 28) only starts 2000. 1988–96 interpolated from annual snapshots; 1997–99 from real quarterly general-government net debt (MDK2) — both scaled ×1.12, a factor empirically validated against 26 years of real overlap data (2000–2026, incl. the full QE era) but still an approximation for the years it's applied to.
θ calibrated using Bank Rate, not a market short rate
No UK 3-month T-bill/gilt series was sourced (the US build uses USGG3M, a market instrument distinct from the Fed funds policy rate). Bank Rate substitutes for it here — a real deviation, not a clean equivalent. θ = 1.41% vs. the US's −0.39%; not yet fully explained (see chat history).
No second (core/trimmed) inflation measure
Headline CPI only. No Dallas-style trimmed-mean equivalent exists off the shelf for the UK.
Next OBR Economic and Fiscal Outlook
Twice yearly (~March, ~November). Refreshes the output-gap series (Table 1.14) and CPI/GDP forecasts.
Unlike the US tab's dynamic countdown banner, this is a static summary — no live date-driven refresh logic has been built for the UK model yet.
