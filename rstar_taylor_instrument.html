Static historical series from the full model build (headline PCE, our per-capita-growth r*). The live panels above are a scenario tool for the current-quarter starting point — moving the sliders does not redraw this chart. Hover to inspect any year.
Model: US neutral rate (r*) built from per-capita trend growth (HP-filtered), demographic (FRED/World Bank dependency ratio), fiscal (Bloomberg FEDDHPBP), and current-account (Bloomberg EHCAUS) adjustment terms, θ calibrated against 1985–2007 realized real rates net of known drivers. Taylor Rule uses CBO potential GDP for the output gap and PCE (BEA) for inflation. Cross-checked against the NY Fed Holston-Laubach-Williams r* series. Built entirely from public/licensed data — see accompanying repo for full derivation and source notebooks.
